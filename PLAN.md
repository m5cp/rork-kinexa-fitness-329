# Soften the logo's hard black edges on the opening screen

**What will change on the splash screen**

- The sharp rectangular edges of the K logo's black box will fade smoothly into the dark background, so the logo appears to float rather than sit inside a visible square.
- A soft radial fade will be applied around the logo so the outer edges blend seamlessly, while the K mark itself stays crisp and fully visible in the center.
- A subtle gold glow around the logo will be preserved to keep the cinematic feel.
- No other part of the splash animation, timing, or layout will change.
