# Remove the Recipes section

**What will be removed:**
- The "AI Recipes" card from the Nutrition tab
- The Recipes screen, recipe detail view, and generate-recipe sheet
- All recipe-related data models and behind-the-scenes logic
- Mentions of "recipes" in the upgrade/upsell screen (will be replaced with other existing nutrition features)

**What stays the same:**
- All nutrition logging (manual entry, barcode, text, AI food scan, repeat meals, meal templates)
- Daily nutrition totals and macro tracking
- Daily AI scan limits
- Everything else in the app is untouched

**Result:**
The Nutrition tab becomes cleaner and more focused on logging food. No recipe browsing or AI recipe generation anywhere in the app.
